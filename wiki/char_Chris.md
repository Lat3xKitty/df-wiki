## Chris Melrose
The vice-president of the university in Swindon's outskirts. She recently started working there, and most of the other teachers and staff members in the university treat her as the "errand girl", making her do tasks and duties that are really her responsibility. She doesn't really mind that, because she likes to help the students that attend that university.

Chris very strict when working. She knows that when she need to get something done, she's going to try her best get the best possible result. Although, she is easy-going when there's no need for a perfect result.

She is a very good friend of [Kate](./char_Kate.md), even though they only meet on occasional weekends. She knows her very well, to the point that she can tell what Kate could be thinking about by only looking at her face. Chris has known for a long time that Kate has a crush on her, but she's waiting for Kate to muster the courage to ask her out. She doesn't know that Kate is actually Light.

On her days off, she enjoys reading superhero comics most of the time, she even sometimes writes some stories herself that she will never publish out of fear.

## Sun Soldier Sol
This is Chris' form after being transformed into a superhero by the unknown entity made out of light. Her body shape has changed in order to protect her real identity from being discovered, meaning her chest, waist, hair, and eyes are completely different compared to her original form.

Her abilities are based on everything related to fire, from incinerating to explosions. Since she is very new to this whole world of superhero abilities, she can't control them very well, but she is constantly trying her best to develop her abilities to defeat evil and save the weak. She has very strong abilities, but since she is pretty new to using them, she tends to waste a lot of her energy while using them, making her feel weak after activating a couple of them without managing her energy.

Sol's flames are very special. They can neutralize and cleanse Leena's corruptive energy, meaning that if a Slave of Pleasure or Slave Maiden is burnt by her flame, that person will be cleansed and freed from Leena's control. Apart from that special effect on Leena's energy, her flames also behave like normal fire. Because of this, she sometimes feels bad when she burns other villains or Leena's slaves, because she is afraid of hurting them with her flames.

She is now Light's partner in defending the UK. Kate helps her train as a superhero and how to behave like one, since Sol is kind of reluctant about announcing her attacks like all other superheroes do.

## Chris' Necklace
The catalyst to activate Sol's transformation and an accessory that also prevents Chris from being corrupted.

With the same function as Kate's star-shaped earrings, Chris can activate the necklace to trigger her transformation into Sol. She always wears it, and she can never lose it. Even if she takes it off, it will always come back to her immediately, as if a ghost is putting it around her neck.

Whenever Chris is being corrupted or being affected by a corruptive influence, the necklace activates and imbues Chris' body with Purifying Flames to prevent her from,falling control of said influence or corruption. This is, of course, wants to resist this corruption or influence that is trying to dominate her. If she were to give in willingly with all her heart, the neckless would not deny her wish to be corrupted and/or dominated by that influence.

If Chris was transformed or forced to be transformed into some kind of form that attempted to corrupt her by force, the necklace would enter Chris' body to constantly prevent the corruptive energy from spreading to her brain. Even after this scenario, Chris would be able to transform into Sol freely, as she doesn't need would be able to physically interact with the necklace itself to transform.

## Slave Maiden Chris
Latex Maidens are not the same as Slave Maidens, the only difference between the two is who that person was before turning into a Slave Maiden. A normal woman can only become a Slave Maiden, but a superhero or someone with some kind of super power will become a Latex Maiden. Latex Maidens have the same appearance as Slave Maidens at, first, but provided by her Mistress Leena, their appearance and powers will to Leena's wishes.Those powers will definitely be designed to help Leena dominate Earth.

As per Chris, her mind has been modified and brainwashed to believe that she is a Latex Maiden. She will obey all orders that Leena tells her to to do, and nothing will stop her from fulfilling an order. The worst thing (or best, if you're on Leena's side) is that Chris can still turn into Sol, allowing her to disguise as a superhero and do whatever she wants while no one suspects that she's actually Leena's Latex Maiden.

As a Latex Maiden, she has the objective of turning people into Leena's Slaves by any means possible, whether that be by dominating the victim, tricking the victim, or even by making the victim want to become a slave willingly.

Unlike Slave Maidens, Chris can turn back to her old "uncorrupted" self, because she needs to hide from Light. If Light were to discover that Sol was corrupted, she would try to save Sol. Or even worse, defeat her to prevent her from spreading Leena's corruption.
