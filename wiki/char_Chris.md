## Chris Melrose
The vice-president of the university in Swindon's outskirts. She recently started working there, and most of the other teachers and staff members in the university treat her as the "errand girl", making her do tasks and duties that are really her responsibility. She doesn't really mind that, because she likes to help the students that attend that university.

Chris very strict when working. She knows that when she need to get something done, she's going to try her best get the best possible result. Although, she is easy-going when there's no need for a perfect result.

She is a very good friend of [Kate](./char_Kate.md), even though they only meet on occasional weekends. She knows her very well, to the point that she can tell what Kate could be thinking about by only looking at her face. Chris has known for a long time that Kate has a crush on her, but she's waiting for Kate to muster the courage to ask her out. She doesn't know that Kate is actually Light.

On her days off, she enjoys reading superhero comics most of the time, she even sometimes writes some stories herself that she will never publish out of fear.

## Sun Soldier Sol
This is Chris' form after being transformed into a superhero by the unknown entity made out of light. Her body shape has changed in order to protect her real identity from being discovered, meaning her chest, waist, hair, and eyes are completely different compared to her original form.

Her abilities are based on everything related to fire, from incinerating to explosions. Since she is very new to this whole world of superhero abilities, she can't control them very well, but she is constantly trying her best to develop her abilities to defeat evil and save the weak. She has very strong abilities, but since she is pretty new to using them, she tends to waste a lot of her energy while using them, making her feel weak after activating a couple of them without managing her energy.

Sol's flames are very special. They can neutralize and cleanse Leena's corruptive energy, meaning that if a Slave of Pleasure or Slave Maiden is burnt by her flame, that person will be cleansed and freed from Leena's control. Apart from that special effect on Leena's energy, her flames also behave like normal fire. Because of this, she sometimes feels bad when she burns other villains or Leena's slaves, because she is afraid of hurting them with her flames.

She is now Light's partner in defending the UK. Kate helps her train as a superhero and how to behave like one, since Sol is kind of reluctant about announcing her attacks like all other superheroes do.
