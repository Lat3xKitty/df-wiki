## Leena
A very sadistic and cruel mistress that comes from another planet. She once ruled a whole planet, having all kinds,of slaves serving under her and playing with them all the time. All of that disappeared when someone attacked her planet and defeated her. She now works and helps them conquer other planets in exchange of having the
opportunity to own slaves whenever she conquers a planet.

Leena has such a dominant spirit, meaning she can bend the will of anyone she looks at in the eyes. If someone stares at her eyes when she's looking at them, they will feel such an immense pressure that can make those who have a weak will or a subduing personality fall immediatelyfor her, allowing her to turn the majority of people she finds into her slaves.

Her whip is not a tool for her, the whip is a part of her being. She can spawn it at will and, can make her dominant energy travel through it.This helps her apply her "Pain Marks on her victims. Those marks keep making the user feel the same pain they felt when Leena whipped them, and after that, they will also feel a slight burst of pleasure to slowly make the victim want to feel the pain for the pleasure.

Leena can also focus her energy into her hands, making it radiate some kind of electricity. This electricity is designed in a way that will only trigger all the pain receptors/on the victim's body, making them feel an intense pain to wherever Leena is applying this electricity. This works even better on her slaves since their body already has Leena's corruptive energy, making it easier to spread the painful electricity.

## Her Influence
Leena's influence is no joke. Upon the first moment that she set foot on Earth, her presence spread to everywhere in the UK and there's nothing that can stop it from affecting people.

This influence, as explained before, can turn weak-minded or submissive people into her slaves easily, or in some other cases, she can appear in someone's mind and lead him or her to turn into a slave, making the person think it's just their imagination or fantasy.

If someone were to start thinking about Leena or start imagining her doing something, and they don't stop, the imaginative Leena will gain sentience and start acting as if they were the real Leena, slowly guiding them to her or to seek out other slaves in order to be captured and turned into slaves themselves. Leena herself is unaware of what these projections do inside the minds of other people, but all of them want the same objective, they want the person to become her slave.

This influence can't really turn anyone into Leena's slave, but can heavily guide them into a situation where they might get corrupted. And since Leena herself has "convinced" the person who is imagining her, the whole process of turning them into a slave is easier.

## Relationship with Cybal
Discussed within Cybal's ending card of Episode 5 of the series, Cybal was one of the people who had joined the Darkness and defeated Leena's own planet forcing her to join the Darkness alliance. This made Cybal think lowly of Leena, leading her to constantly annoy her about it, up to the point where Leena wishes she could kill Cybal very painfully. The stealing of Light and converting her into NEO L1GHT, was another step for taking away something which was once Leena's away from her.
