## Slave Maidens
Slave Maidens are also Leena's slaves, but they are a more dominant type of slave. Since Leena is only one person, she needs slaves to control lower slaves, and that's why she makes Slave Maidens. Slave Maidens act like a mistress in front of Slaves of Pleasure, to control them and give orders. But that's not the only thing the Slave Maidens do, they can perfectly carry out orders from Leena to capture other people or scout for other potential Slave Maidens. Their bodies have also been enhanced, but their suit also enhances pleasure instead of only pain. This is because Leena loves to make Slave Maidens reach climax, but deny them from cumming at the end.

## Latex Maidens
Latex Maidens are not the same as Slave Maidens, the only difference between the two is who that person was before turning into a Slave Maiden. A normal woman can only become a Slave Maiden, but a superhero or someone with some kind of super power will become a Latex Maiden. Latex Maidens have the same appearance as Slave Maidens at, first, but provided by her Mistress Leena, their appearance and powers will to Leena's wishes.Those powers will definitely be designed to help Leena dominate Earth.

Their mind are modified and brainwashed to believe that they are a Latex Maiden. They'll obey all orders that Leena tells her to to do, and nothing will stop her from fulfilling an order. As a Latex Maiden, They still have the same objectives of turning people into Leena's Slaves by any means possible, whether that be by dominating the victim, tricking the victim, or even by making the victim want to become a slave willingly.
