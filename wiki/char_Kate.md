## Kate Godwin
A young and kind woman that has a big righteous spirit, who always helps anyone in need. She is very shy person, although that doesn't stop her from sometimes helping those who could expose her in front of a crowd and garner some unwanted attention.

She is currently working at the Miller as a waitress. She likes to work there a lot since the customers are usually kind to her, and she gets to train herself in being a bit more brave when talking to other people. If you get to know her, you would realize she is caring for her friends and close ones, and will try her best to cheer her friends up most of the time.

She was 26 years old when she was presented with the choice of becoming a superhero. She hesitated a little bit, but in the end she was too excited to even give it a second thought. She was transformed into a superhero and given those starry earrings that help her transform. She's able to transform without them, but it would require a lot more energy to do so and it would take a longer time to do it.

Her hobbies include playing video games and being lazy (When being off-duty): She plays difficult games most of the time to help her vent about how terrible and exploiting her boss is, more than once has she imagined the boss she's killing in the game being her actual boss.

She has a crush for Chris, the vice-principal of the university in Swindon's outskirts. She always liked women because men kind of scare her. And for Kate, Chris is the perfect woman that she would love to take care of. Her fantasy is to live alongside her.


## Star Knight Light
The greatest superhero inthe whole of the UK. She has been defending it from all kinds of villains, from evil scientists to giant monsters. She's managed to win every single one of those encounters with her magical star powers.

Her range of abilities are almost limitless, but she can only manifest it through her body, which means her range is rather short and limited to melee combat. But whenever she gets into contact range, almost no one can resist one of her "Star" abilities.

Her most used ability is called "Star Crusher". She concentrates energy into her fist and punches her enemy, which then phases through them and blows up outwards, sending them flying away and leaving the victim out of combat most of the time.

In order to detransform, she has to use "Starlight Purifier" to cleanse her whole body from everything that is not her human form. This helps her control her powers easily and cleanse her body from any posion and/or ailment she might have, alongside any tracking the enemy mighttve put on her.

After absorbing Leena's binders' Light's appearance changed a little bit to fit her superhero outfit preferences. Her hair became a longer ponytail, she has glossy polished nails and the binders have been permanently attached to her neck. After Sol's purifying flame, the binders no longer make her more susceptible to be corrupted by Leena, but they will still make her have highly erotic dreams.
