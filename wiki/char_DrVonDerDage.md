The daughter of a marriage of scientists. Some would say that she was "created", but no one knows for sure. Ever since she was a kid, she was highly intelligent. She had the mind of a scholar at the age of 6, and it didn't look like her intelligence would stop growing.

As time passed, her dislike towards what she call "single brain-celled people" grew more and more, up to the point that she built a whole immense underground facility to experiment in peace, with her robots and other tools. But this took a bit of a wrong turn after she got curious in the field of "Human Experimentation".

At the age of 20, she started constructing robots designed to kidnap people which she would then perform experiments on. These experiments were neither painful nor deadly, they were more about the exploration of the human limits regarding pleasure. She made people feel pleasure in many different ways to see what would be the best way to feel pleasure, so she would be able to enjoy it the most.

After a couple months of experimenting, Von Der Dage found out that she was about to run out of funds for her experiments and research. Right after that, for better or for worse, Rim contacted her to hire her for her "Superhero Program". Von Der Dage was furious and enraged, she always hated superheroes for their "moral" and "good intentions". She accepted anyway, because she didn't have money for anything, not even for food. Having signed the contract, Von Der Dage had to stop experimenting with people, and use her genius mind to design and manufacture technology for Rim's company, in order to help her and her superheroes solve their "superproblems".

Doctor Von Der Dage is the creator of B.O.B. (Best of Bots) who was a complete mistake. Some errors in her Calculations caused her to accidentally create a sentient AI. While B.O.B. is annoying, due to his dumb and naive brain, she's slowly started to grow attached to B.O.B.
