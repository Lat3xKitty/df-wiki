## Sex Drones
Like Leena, Cybal has her own collection of minions to help inact on her bidding. However rather than having Slaves with their own minds, Cybal specifically has full control over her Drones. All of Cybal's Drones are females, and that's the only way she will gave them, if she was ever to find a male to become a Drone of her's, it's more than likely Cybal will convert them into a Female.

Cybal's was programmed to analyze brainwaves of any type of creature she negages with and will find whatever is the easiest path to break its mind and gain control over it through brainwashing it. It starts off with understanding common brain wavelengths coming from the brain, and understand what makes them tick, to then break them. In the case of humans, she simply needs to make them feel pleasure.

Sex Drones use Nano bots to help modify and enchance them, making them faster, stronger and tougher versions. Intestingly these Drones are able to to hide the fact they are drones by concealing the brainwashing device down their throats and not displaying any of the nano drones allowing them to easily camouflage in and attack their next prey.
