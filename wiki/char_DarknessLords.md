This entity is Leena and Cybal's boss. The two work for them, and in exchange this entity doesn't kill them both. The sole purpose of this entity is to conquer as many planets as it can under its name to rule them under its oppressive and corruptive-mantle. No one knows where this entity originated, but everyone who "works" for them knows that this entity is cometing against its counterpart, the Light Mistresses.

When this entity has chosen its objective to conquer, it sends its employees to dominate its population, and then claim the planet in the property of Darkness. When doing this, the strongest person or champion on that planet is then "hired" by the Darkness Lords, and they immediately start working for them, whether they want it or not.

Although those who work for the Darkness Lords use the terms of "working for them", it's' because they have no choice. They either die a terrible and painful death, or conquer planets for them. In exchange, the Darkness Lords can provide eternal life (Not immortality) to its employees. For a lot of people, this offer was very tempting. Leena and Cybal are very aware of the temper and wrath of the Darkness Lords, so they obey their orders despite hating to obey them.
