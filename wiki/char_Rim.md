## Rim Stone
An old superhero who has been secretly retired from service. She was once called Crimson Blade, the strongest superhero the United Kingdom has ever known. Her prowess with the sword is still unmatched even after all this time and her strength is second to none.

20 years ago, Rim got kidnapped by Dr. Nexus, who then fell in love with her. Luckily for him, this love was mutual. After many months of bureaucracy, Rim and Dr. Nexus managed to convince the government to allow them to fake their deaths and retire as superheroes, so they could enjoy the rest of their lives together. Although, Dr. Nexus' machines operated off nuclear energy, so he didn't live for many more years after retiring.

After Dr. Nexus' passing, Rim focused her efforts on finding a way to end this pointless conflict of villains versus heroes, and her idea to solve this conflict was with money. Since her husband passed away, she got all his fortunes. And with the money she was being paid to defend the UK's greatest enemies, she started hiring heroes and villains alike, paying them to use their powers to get more money. One example of that is Dr Von Der Dage, Rim-hired her to use her technological skills to invent new and sophisticated devices for people to buy.

The briefcase she is carrying was made by Dr. Nexus, it could be roughly described as a "pocket dimension", you can put anything you want in it and pull anything you want out of it. That is of course, if it fits through the hole of the briefcase. This was the last invention that Dr. Nexus made for Rim, and she plans to keep it forever with her and never give it to anyone else.
