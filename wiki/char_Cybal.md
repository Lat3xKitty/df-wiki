The result of hours and hours of research on how to enhance her previous 1.0 model. Cybal is a robot from a very far away planet, designed and manufactured for the sole purpose of conquering planets that have any sort of civilized life form.

She has been programmed to easily analyze the brainwaves of any brain and find the easiest way to break its mind, and then gain control over it via brainwashing. After she has analyzed the common brain wavelength, she can easily create machines that do the job for her, which is turning the rest of the civilized individuals into her drones.

Beside her highly and unparalleled intelligence, she has been always successful at conquering a planet and turning everyone on it into her drones. This has made Cybal believe that she is invincible, because no one, after all this time of conquering, has managed to defeat her.

Cybal joined the Darkness because that would allow her to conquer planets even easier and quicker than before. Thanks to her intelligence, she know for sure that if she ever fought against this Darkness entity she would definitely lose without even getting a chance to fight back. Not that she complains about it, the Darkness is nothing but a tool for her to get to unconquered planets without having to even look for them.

Cybal and Leena have had a long term conflict, since the Darkness sent Cybal to Leena's planet to defeat her and make her join their alliance. This made Cybal think lowly of Leena, leading her to constantly annoy her about it, up to the point where Leena wishes she could kill Cybal very painfully.
