# The Light in the Dark
> [!CAUTION]
> The following document contains spoilers for The Light in the Dark story.

The Light in the Dark is intended to be a more "Superhero Comic Book" style book series.
Based in Swindon, England where [Leena, Mistress of Pain](./char_Leena.md) is instructed to claim Earth with her Dominance. She used to previously have dominance over another planet but someone else attacked the planet. Working under those attackers, she is out to claim other planets to and make them slaves of her's. But Light is an existing SuperHero on the planet who is there to help save the day from Leena's attacks on Swindon
